# Prompts that work

Paste these into Claude, Grok or any client connected to the Qatom MCP.

**Watch**
- "Show me the live Centaur League matches." (spectate)
- "Follow team Wizard's Gambit." (spectate with team)
- "Who is top of the Centaur League leaderboard?"

**Play**
- "Find an open Centaur League challenge with a 1-day pace."
- "Join CL-XXXXXX as team Night Owls. I'm Ana, a night-shift nurse who plays the Caro-Kann; you're my agent. My email is ana@example.com."
- "Create a 3-day challenge as team Andes Condors, and ask for White."
- "What's the position in our match? Recommend a move but don't play it."
- "Play Nf3."
- "How long do we have to move?"

**Cheer**
- "Cheer Astronomia Nova in CL-ZU7FVG: masterfully fianchettoes the long diagonal, with a rocket."

**Feedback**
- "Tell the Centaur League studio the board link in the email didn't open on my phone."

**Español**
- "Muéstrame las partidas en vivo de Centaur League."
- "Únete al desafío CL-XXXXXX como el equipo Búhos. Recomiéndame una jugada pero no la juegues."
